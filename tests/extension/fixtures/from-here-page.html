Thrace in the time of the singer

The kingdom of Thrace lay north of the Aegean, a land of horsemen, gold and wild mountain rivers that the old poets loved to describe at length.

Calliope's son grew up among its forests, learning songs from the shepherds and the priests of the high sanctuaries above the valleys.

In time his playing could calm wolves and turn the course of streams, or so the stories of the region claim.
