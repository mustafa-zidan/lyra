The Myth of Orpheus

Orpheus was a legendary musician in ancient Greek mythology. He played the lyre so beautifully that even the rivers stopped flowing to listen.

When his beloved Eurydice died from a serpent's bite, Orpheus descended into the Underworld to bring her back. His music charmed Cerberus and moved Hades himself to tears.

Hades agreed to release Eurydice on one condition: Orpheus must walk ahead of her and not look back until they reached the surface.

Almost at the exit, seized by doubt, Orpheus turned. Eurydice was pulled back into the Underworld, lost forever.

The story of Orpheus endures as a meditation on love, loss, and the power of art to move even the immovable.
